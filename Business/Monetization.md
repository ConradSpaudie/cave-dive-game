# Monetization

"Monetization" means how the game makes money.

## Decided
- The game itself is free.
- Paid DLC later is okay.
- Ads were floated as the main revenue source.

## Recommendation
Free game, paid cosmetics. Specifically:
- Free base game on Steam.
- Paid cosmetic packs: wetsuit designs, mask styles, flipper styles, lanterns, hologram watch skins.
- A one-time "Supporter Pack" for fans who want to back the project.
- Nothing paid that makes you stronger. Pay-to-win kills co-op games.

Why not ads: in-game ads are rare on Steam and PC players strongly dislike them. They earn very little per player and would hurt reviews, and reviews are what make a free Steam game spread.

The hologram watch map could be a cosmetic upgrade (fancier hologram), but the basic map should stay free because it's a core navigation tool.

## Open
- Final decision on ads vs cosmetics.
