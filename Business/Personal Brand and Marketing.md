# Personal Brand and Marketing

## Why this project exists
- This game is the launch of Conrad's personal brand on Instagram.
- It's how Conrad gets good at talking on camera, editing, and pitching.
- Conrad and his friends are the faces of the brand, not a faceless studio.
- The bar: the game has to be genuinely great.

## Plan
- Build in public: devlogs on Instagram and TikTok from day one.
- Teaser trailer before launch.
- Public demo / playtest on Steam before full launch.
- Reach out to streamers once the demo is fun (PEAK-style games spread through streamers).
