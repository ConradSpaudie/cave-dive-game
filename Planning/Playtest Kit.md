# Playtest Kit (Phase 1, solo)

Goal of this playtest: find out if the dive *feels* good and never gets repetitive. Not bugs first, feelings first.

## Before you hand it out
1. Pull the latest code, close the editor, build, open, press Play once and swim the whole route (`dive.GoToMark` lists every stop).
2. Run `dive.ResetHints` so testers see the first-dive hint cards.
3. Package a Mac build: Platforms > Mac > Package Project, pick an empty folder (for example ~/Builds/TiedUnder_Phase1). The first package takes a long time (shaders). Zip the .app and send it.
4. Windows testers need a Windows PC to package on. Skip them for this round.

## What to tell testers (copy this)
"This is a first test of how cave diving feels. Play it solo, headphones on, lights off in your room. Do one full dive: swim to the end and back. Don't read any controls first, the game should teach you. Afterwards answer 6 quick questions."

## The 6 questions
1. What was the most fun moment? What was the most boring one?
2. Where were you confused about what to press or where to go?
3. Did you ever feel scared or tense? Where?
4. Did swimming feel heavy and real, or floaty, or stiff?
5. Did you want to dive again right away? Why or why not?
6. One thing you would change.

## What to watch for (if you can sit next to them)
- The first 60 seconds: do they find the light and the vest (B and V) on their own?
- The squeeze and the chimney: do they panic, get stuck, or laugh?
- The chamber: do they stop and look around? That is the "wow" moment.
- Air: do they turn back in time, or drown? Both are fine, note which.

## After
Put the answers in a new note, Planning/Playtest 1 Results.md, one section per tester. Look for anything 3 or more people said.
