# Roadmap

The full design is huge. At 10 hours a week, trying to build everything at once means nothing ever gets finished. The plan is to build one small piece that looks and feels amazing, then grow it.

## Phase 0: Setup (week 1)
- Get an external SSD and free up space.
- Install Unreal Engine 5 and Xcode on the SSD.
- Create the GitHub repo with this vault inside it.
- Confirm access to a Windows PC for builds.

## Phase 1: "The Feel" prototype (weeks 2 to 6)
One handmade tunnel, solo only.
- First-person swimming with visible hands.
- Flashlight with darkness that falls off fast around corners.
- Air meter and breathing audio.
- Buoyancy by breathing into the device.
- Silt-out effect.
Goal: swimming through one dark tunnel feels incredible. Record it and post it. This is devlog #1.

## Phase 2: Friends (weeks 7 to 12)
- 2-player online co-op via Steam.
- The rope connecting both players.
- Proximity voice.
- Grabbing each other.
Goal: two people swimming together on a rope is already funny.

## Phase 3: The cave (weeks 13 to 20)
- Modular rock kit and the first procedural generator.
- Branching paths, air pockets, a dry chamber.
- Currents and the rescue mini game.

## Phase 4: The run (weeks 21 to 28)
- Depth effects, panic, injuries, tape.
- First creatures and the logbook.
- Reach-the-bottom goal and end screen.
- Hub v1.

## Phase 5: Playtest demo
- Steam page, teaser trailer, free playtest.

Later: 4 players, saboteur, customization editors, hologram map, leaderboards, upgrades, earthquakes, more creatures.
