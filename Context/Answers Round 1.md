## Players and multiplayer
12. 1 to 4 players to start.
13. Playable solo. Separate solo and multiplayer modes.
14. Friends-only at first; public matchmaking later (voice chat with strangers makes it complicated).
15. Built-in proximity voice, with a toggle so players can use Discord or FaceTime instead.
16. Sabotage, only in 4-player games: one player is secretly the saboteur whose goal is to ruin the mission or kill everyone.
17. Death handling: not answered.
18. Join mid-dive: yes. The rope runs from a stake outside the cave through every player; a late joiner spawns at the top and follows the rope down.

## Tone and feel
19. Mostly funny chaos with some horror and tense realism: 85% funny, 15% tense/horror. Everything should still look realistic. "Funny" to be defined later.
20. Real cave-style wildlife, prehistoric fish deeper down, new animals unlocked at depth, darker as you go, bioluminescent creatures like anglerfish. Invented creatures too.
21. About 90% gameplay. A short intro story about how the divers got here, then players drive it.
22. Some gore and jump scares are fine (earthquakes, getting cut on rocks by fast water). Not "all kitty."
23. Copy PEAK's vibe: funny with friends, plus a clear goal (PEAK climbs up). Aesthetic of a dark cave diving horror game, keeping the funniness.

## Movement and physics
24. Fully first person. No third person.
25. Swimming must be the most refined thing in the game.
26. Buoyancy by breathing into the device: a button puts your mouth on it to inflate; breathe it back in to deflate and recover some air. Air refills at air pockets and dry areas.
27. Crawl, squeeze, pull, and push modes, usable in dry sections and in underwater holes.
28. Players can grab each other. Rope plus grabbing the wall lets you save a teammate caught in a current, via a rescue mini game; fail and everyone gets dragged.
29. Hands visible at the screen edges while swimming and grabbing.
30. Ragdoll when falling from height in dry sections, with fall damage.
31. Dry sections: yes.
32. Currents: yes.

## Diving realism
33. Depth matters: less stamina, less breath held, reduced function, foggy vision that looks beautiful and intentional.
34. Air pockets hinted with glass-like indicators that appear at medium range and scale with distance.
35. Not answered directly.
36. Not answered directly.
37. Carry extra tanks with trade-offs (more tanks, less stamina). Three loadout options. Drop tanks to regain stamina and come back for them like checkpoints.
38. Panic: rises from cuts and currents, lowers by waiting it out, drains stamina and function. Repair cuts with duct tape or Flex Tape.
39. Gear fails: flashlights die, strong currents flood or knock off masks. Regulator failure undecided.
40. Wrist dive computer with a holographic 3D cave map. Maybe a paid feature later, free for now.
