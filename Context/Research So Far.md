# Research So Far (2026-10-17)

## Market gap
No first-person, actually-underwater, tense-but-funny co-op cave diving game has broken out. Existing games are close but different.

## On Roblox: "cave diving" means dry crawling
- **Expedition Cave Dive**: team or solo crawl through caves and biomes to the "world's deepest cave." About 2 million visits, all-time peak of 3,260 concurrent players in mid-October 2026.
- **Cave Diving Experience**: released April 2024, 3.3 million+ visits, mostly crawling and squeezing with some flooded sections.
- Takeaway: proven demand for cave content, but nobody is doing it underwater and realistic.

## On Steam / itch: small underwater co-op games, none are hits
- **Deep**: up to 4-player co-op diving, oxygen management, sharing oxygen. Silly, arcade style. Closest competitor.
- **Hadalis**: co-op deep sea survival in a fragile submersible. Sub, not diver.
- **Wreck Below**: 1 to 4 player co-op horror, diving into haunted wrecks.
- **Mindaro**: 1 to 6 player co-op action-horror descent.
- **Hermit Cave** (game jam): diver + navigator split roles.
- **Breath Bound** (game jam, 2D): two divers sharing one air supply, "the way up is harder than the way down."
- **Subnautica 2**: 4-player co-op underwater survival, open world rather than a descent run.

## What makes this game different
- First person, photorealistic, actually underwater.
- One rope connecting the whole team.
- Real cave diving mechanics: silt-outs, buoyancy by breath, depth effects, squeezes.
- PEAK-style friend chaos.
