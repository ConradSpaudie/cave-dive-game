# Cave Dive Game (working title)

First-person, photorealistic, 1 to 4 player co-op cave diving game for Steam, built in Unreal Engine 5. The feel is PEAK (funny chaos with friends, a goal at the end of the run) flipped upside down: you descend into an endless, procedurally generated flooded cave instead of climbing a mountain.

Built by Conrad and Tyrus with Claude Code. One Mac, one Claude subscription, about 10 hours a week, no deadline.

## The one-line pitch
PEAK, but you're cave diving, tied to your friends by one rope, and the deeper you go the stranger it gets.

## The failure condition
If the game becomes repetitive, it has failed. Every system should be judged against this.
