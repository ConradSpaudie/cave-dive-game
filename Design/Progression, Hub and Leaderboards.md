# Progression, Hub and Leaderboards

## Goal of a run
- Main goal: reach the bottom.
- Side goals: log creatures and plants, bring items back to the surface, dig up ancient bones and treasure.
- Treasure sells for money used to buy and upgrade gear.

## Ending
- There is an ending. At the end players choose: **End** (finish) or **Keep going** (endless replay).

## Leaderboards
- Deepest dive
- Fastest run to the end
- Most air left
- Most explored (measured by rope used)
- Longest time spent in the cave
