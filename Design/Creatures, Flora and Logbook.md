# Creatures, Flora and Logbook

## Decided
- Wildlife changes with depth. Shallower areas have more familiar life; deeper areas have prehistoric and stranger creatures.
- Deep zones have bioluminescent creatures (anglerfish style) that act as the main light source once your flashlight dies.
- Electric eels.
- Invented creatures (to be designed later).
- Glowing moss (rare) and other plants and fungi that can be collected and logged.
- Ancient bones and fossils of long-lost creatures that can be dug up and brought back as treasure.

## Bioluminescent lantern
- Buy a cage or lantern (aesthetic, rendered in UE5).
- Catch bioluminescent creatures in it. Each species has its own brightness. More creatures means more light.
- Becomes your light source when your flashlight battery runs out.

## Logbook
- Waterproof logbook.
- Bring measurement tools to measure creatures and plants and record their size.
- Goal: tens and tens of species to discover.

## Species ideas (starter list, mix of real and game fiction)
Real cave life the designs can be based on:
- **Cave mosses near entrances and light**, such as Eucladium verticillatum, which colonizes stalagmites in show caves.
- **Ferns near entrances**, such as maidenhair spleenwort (Asplenium trichomanes).
- **Algae films** on wet rock near light.
- **Cave fungi** on wood and organic debris, often deformed by the darkness.
- **Bioluminescent fungi** (real glowing species exist, like Mycena chlorophos), good basis for glowing moss and shelf fungi.
- **Glowworms** hanging from cave ceilings (New Zealand's Waitomo caves are famous for these). Great for dry chambers.
- **Blind cavefish** (no eyes, pale pink skin).
- **The olm**, a blind, pale cave salamander that can live a very long time.
- **Cave shrimp and cave crayfish**, pale and nearly transparent.
- **Remipedes**, ancient venomous cave crustaceans found in flooded caves.
- **Bacterial mats** that look like slime or cloth on the cave floor.

Prehistoric and fictional (deep zones):
- Coelacanth-style "living fossil" fish.
- Anglerfish-style lure predators (anglerfish actually live in the deep ocean, not caves, which is fine for game fiction).
- Giant isopods.
- Electric eels.
- Invented: TBD.
