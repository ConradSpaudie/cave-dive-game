# Cave Generation

## Decided
- Fully fictional cave, not based on a real place.
- Procedurally generated. Thousands of different caves and passages. Every cave has its own character.
- It should feel endless without feeling repetitive. There is an ending, but it should feel like it goes forever.
- Extremely high detail. A low-detail cave is a failure.
- Branching paths. Choosing a path is mostly one-way: going back is very hard because the last person has to lead the crawl back out.
- Some branches reconnect. Example: five routes, two merge and lead somewhere new, three go their own way.
- Gets dark fast. Turn a corner away from the entrance light and it drops off sharply. Deep zones are pitch black except for bioluminescence.
- Mix of flooded passages, tight squeezes, currents, air pockets, and dry chambers (small and large).
- Silty areas cause silt-outs (see [[Diving Systems]]).
- Some areas are warmer than others.
- Cave collapses and earthquakes can happen and change the route. To be designed later.

## Run length
- Standard run: 30 to 45 minutes.
- Easy difficulty: about 15 minutes.
- Larger groups / harder: 45 minutes to an hour or more.
