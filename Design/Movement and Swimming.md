# Movement and Swimming

## Decided
- Fully first person. Hands and arms visible at the edges of the screen while swimming, grabbing, and pulling. Not overwhelming, but always present.
- Swimming is the single most important feel in the game. It should be the most refined system in the project.
- Multiple movement modes the player can toggle between:
  - Swim (open water)
  - Crawl
  - Squeeze (tight holes, underwater and in dry sections)
  - Pull (drag yourself along rock)
  - Push (push off surfaces)
- Dry sections exist (small and large air pockets). Players can walk, climb, and crawl through them.
- Ragdoll falls in dry sections: falling from enough height ragdolls you and deals damage.
- Players can grab each other (rescue from currents, tow, help through squeezes).
- Players can grab the wall to anchor themselves, for example when a teammate on the rope is being dragged by a current.
- Currents and flow push players around.

## Buoyancy (decided)
- Buoyancy is controlled by breathing into your buoyancy device.
- Press a button to put your mouth on the device and exhale into it: more buoyant, but you spend air.
- To lose buoyancy, put your mouth back on it and breathe the air back in. This also gives you back some breath.

## Rescue mini game (decided, details open)
- When a roped teammate is caught in a current, others grab the wall and pull.
- A short mini game decides whether you save them or everyone gets dragged.
