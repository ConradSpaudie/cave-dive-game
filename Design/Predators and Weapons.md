# Predators and Weapons

Added Oct 19, 2026 from Conrad's playtest feedback:
> "I want you to add the creatures, but I also want to add predators too. And then you should be able to choose from a plethora of weapons at the beginning, like maybe a harpoon gun or a water gun or something. Shoot them down."

## Rules
- Predators are dangerous but beatable. Every attack is telegraphed (glowing eyes, hiss or clicks, body posture) so the player can react.
- Weapons are not a power fantasy: limited ammo, slow reloads, and some cost your tank air or battery. This keeps pillar 5: going down is a choice with a cost.
- Bleeding attracts predators (they smell blood), so tape matters even more.
- Loud weapons scare prey and can attract some predators.
- Some gore is fine (blood clouds, bites), consistent with the 85% funny / 15% horror tone.

## Weapons (chosen on the Loadout screen before a dive)
| Weapon | What it does | Cost / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Dive knife | Always carried, short melee cut | Free |
| Pole spear (Hawaiian sling) | Starter thrust weapon, reusable | Free |
| Pneumatic speargun (harpoon) | Shoots a spear on a line, pins and reels targets, spears stick in rock | Limited spears, slow reload |
| Water jet gun | Pressurized jet: knocks predators back, stuns, blows silt, pushes you back | Each blast spends tank air |
| Bang stick (powerhead) | One-shot contact charge for big predators, shockwave | Very few rounds |
| Flare gun | Lights the cave for about a minute, scares predators | Few flares |
| Shock baton / pulse | Area stun | Uses flashlight battery |

Controls: left mouse fire, right mouse aim, Y reload, 4 holster or draw, 5 or mouse wheel to switch to the knife.

## Predators
| Predator | Zone | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Cenote bull shark | 0 to 1 | Patrols open water near the entrance, bumps first, then bites |
| Pale cave barracuda school | 1 to 2 | Fast darts in, slashes, retreats |
| Gulper ambush eel | 2 | Lunges from wall holes, drags you toward its hole |
| Cave lurker | 2 to 3 | Pale salamander-crocodile, stalks when you aren't looking, charges when your light hits it |
| Spider squid pack | 3 | Circles, inks to blind you, grabs and holds |
| The Maw | 4 | Rare deep mini boss, tanky, retreats into darkness, telegraphed charge |

Existing hazards (Lanternjaw, Volt Eel, crayfish) now also have health and can be fought.

## Also from Oct 19 feedback
- Hand movement at normal speed is good. At sprint it felt erratic: the stroke now keeps a steady rhythm, at most 25% faster when sprinting, and kicks no longer snap the arms back to the start of the stroke.
- Sprint is now stronger kicks at a slightly faster pace instead of much faster kicks.

## Status
Built and live in the browser version (Prototypes/tied-under) as of Oct 19.
- Balance: predator health is doubled to match weapon damage (shark 110, barracuda 20, gulper 70, lurker 140, squid 44, The Maw 520). The free pole spear kills a shark in 3 to 4 hits.
- Rookie grace: no predators in the tutorial, and no predators in the first two zones until you finish one dive (easy and standard). Hard has no grace.
- Being grabbed: kicks drop to a quarter; mash A and D to break free.
- Glow sticks (key 3) and true endless depth (Keep Diving opens new cave below the ring: The Hollow, The Root, and deeper) are also in. For the Unreal version these become their own systems after Phase 2 (co-op).
