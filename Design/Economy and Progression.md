# Economy and Progression

> **PROPOSAL, awaiting Conrad's approval.** Nothing here is decided. Built on the working assumptions in [[Proposed Answers]]. Once approved, move the agreed parts into [[Progression, Hub and Leaderboards]] and [[Gear and Customization]], and log it in [[Decision Log]].

## The short version
- **Two currencies.** Cash ($) buys and upgrades gear. Fin Tokens are earned only (never sold for real money) and buy earned cosmetics and flipper tuning.
- **Money comes from four places:** treasure and fossils, logbook entries, depth bonuses, and contracts from the instructor.
- **Seven upgrade tracks, 5 levels each.** A focused player maxes one track in about 12 to 17 runs. Maxing everything takes about 45 to 50 runs (roughly 30 to 35 hours).
- **Upgrades are small on purpose.** The best maxed stat is +50%. A skilled diver with base gear should still beat a clumsy diver with maxed gear. Skill and teamwork matter more than grinding.
- **Real money only buys looks.** See [[Monetization]].

## 1. Currencies

| Currency | Symbol | How you get it | What it buys | Can you pay real money for it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash | $ | Selling treasure and fossils, logbook bounties, depth bonuses, contracts | Gear upgrades, consumables, the lantern, hub upgrades | No |
| Fin Tokens | FT | Logbook milestones, first-time zone reaches, shiny finds, weekly seed placements, contract streaks | Earned cosmetics, flipper shape and width tuning (from [[Gear and Customization]]) | No |

Real-money cosmetic packs are a separate shop (see section 8). They never touch Cash or Fin Tokens.

### How the haul is split in co-op
**Everyone on the team gets the full team haul.** No splitting, no arguing over who grabbed the gold ammonite. To keep solo fair (fewer hands to carry things), payouts are scaled by team size:

| Team size | Payout multiplier per player |
|---|---|
| Solo | 1.5x |
| 2 players | 1.2x |
| 3 players | 1.1x |
| 4 players | 1.0x |

Dead players still get the full share if the team reaches the bottom. A total wipe keeps 25% of the haul's Cash value plus every logbook entry (from [[Proposed Answers]] #27).

## 2. What earns money

### Treasure and fossils (sell prices by tier)
Tiers match the $ ratings in [[Logbook Species List]].

| Tier | Example items | Sell price | Typical zone |
|---|---|---|---|
| $ | Fossil shell fragment, lost diver's junk | $20 to $40 | 1 to 3 |
| $$ | Trilobite plate, cave bear tooth | $60 to $100 | 3 to 5 |
| $$$ | Ground sloth claw, good lost diver's gear | $150 to $250 | 3 to 4 |
| $$$$ | Saber-tooth fang, gomphothere tusk | $400 to $600 | 4 to 5 |
| $$$$$ | Gold ammonite, Platejaw jaw plate | $1,000 to $1,500 | 5 |
| Shiny version of any item | Gold, glowing, or giant | 3x normal price | Any |

Rules:
- Exact price inside the range depends on condition. Dropped, scraped, or broken items sell at the low end (a broken saber-tooth fang sells for 25%).
- Heavy items slow you down (cave bear tooth, tusk). That is the trade-off for their price.
- Ancient human remains are log only, never sold.

### Logbook bounties
Paid by the instructor's research table when you return.

| What you logged | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Very Rare | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New species (first time) | $25 | $60 | $150 | $400 | $1,000 |
| New variant (morph, albino, juvenile) | $10 | $25 | $60 | $150 | n/a |
| New behavior witnessed | $15 | $30 | $75 | $200 | $500 |
| New size record (biggest or smallest you've measured) | $5 | $10 | $25 | $60 | n/a |
| Photo of a dangerous species | +$50 flat on top of the above | | | | |

Shiny creature sightings pay 5x the species bounty once per shiny type.

### Depth bonuses
Paid once per run for the deepest zone the team reached (not stacked).

| Deepest point reached | Bonus per player |
|---|---|
| Zone 1: The Mouth | $50 (the "you tried" bonus) |
| Zone 2: Twilight Throat | $150 |
| Zone 3: The Black Galleries | $350 |
| Zone 4: The Sulfur Deep | $700 |
| Zone 5: The Old Water | $1,200 |
| Reached the bottom chamber | +$1,000 on top of the Zone 5 bonus |
| Each 50 m past the bottom in "Keep going" mode | +$300 |

### Contracts
The instructor posts requests on the camp board (full list in [[Run Variety System]]). Typical rewards: $150 to $800 Cash plus 1 to 3 Fin Tokens. One active contract per run.

### Fin Tokens

| Event | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Every 5 new species logged | 1 FT |
| First time reaching each zone | 2 FT |
| First time reaching the bottom | 5 FT |
| Finding any shiny | 2 FT |
| Finishing a contract | 1 FT (3 FT for hard contracts) |
| Weekly seed: top 25% of your friends list | 2 FT |
| Completing a full zone page in the logbook | 5 FT |

|---|---|---|---|

Playtest check: if a real run pays more than double these numbers, prices are too high. If less than half, players will feel the grind.

## 4. Upgrade tracks

Every player starts at Level 1 (base). Upgrades belong to each player, not the host, so you keep your progress in a friend's lobby. Prices rise steeply so early levels feel quick and the last level feels like an achievement.

### 4.1 Tank capacity
Air per tank at calm breathing. Loadouts (Light 1 tank, Standard 2, Heavy 3) stay the same; each tank holds more.

| Level | Air per tank | Standard loadout (2 tanks) | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | 20 min | Free |
| 2 | 11 min | 22 min | $300 |
| 3 | 12 min | 24 min | $900 |
| 4 | 13.5 min | 27 min | $2,500 |
| 5 | 15 min | 30 min | $6,000 |

### 4.2 Stamina

| Level | Max stamina | Regen speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | 1.0x | Free |
| 2 | 110 | 1.05x | $250 |
| 3 | 120 | 1.1x | $800 |
| 4 | 135 | 1.2x | $2,200 |
| 5 | 150 | 1.3x | $5,500 |

Depth, panic, and extra tanks still drain stamina the same percentage, so deep zones always hurt (see [[Diving Systems]]).

### 4.3 Swim speed

| Level | Swim speed | Sprint speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Base | Base | Free |
| 2 | +4% | +4% | $300 |
| 3 | +8% | +8% | $900 |
| 4 | +12% | +12% | $2,500 |
| 5 | +15% | +15%, sprint kicks up 25% less silt | $6,000 |

Hard cap at +15%. Faster than that and silt-outs and currents stop mattering.

### 4.4 Flashlight battery

| Level | Unlocks | Effect | Cost |

## 5. Cosmetics: earned vs bought

**Rule: the coolest-looking items are earned, not bought.** Paid packs are fun and colorful; earned items are the flex.

| Category | Earned (Fin Tokens or achievements) | Bought (real money) |
|---|---|---|
| Wetsuit | Zone-themed suits (Sulfur Yellow, Old Water Black), Kevin the training dummy sticker, logbook completion patterns | Color packs, sticker packs, themed suits (shark, banana, astronaut) |
| Mask | Lanternjaw-tooth frame, Platejaw-plate frame, tinted glass skins | Frame shapes and colors |
| Flippers | Shape and width tuning (tokens, per [[Gear and Customization]]), "Rope Burn" flippers for 100 runs | Flipper styles and colors |
| Lantern | Starfall lantern skin (catch a Starfall Swarm), gold ammonite lantern | Lantern cage styles |
| Watch | Hologram color for reaching the bottom 10 times | Fancy hologram themes |
| Hub | Trophies, fossil shelves, species sketches (see [[Hub Design]]) | Camp decorations (hammock, flags, radio skins) |

Earned cosmetic prices in Fin Tokens: small items 3 FT, mid items 8 FT, big items 15 FT, prestige items are achievement only.

## 6. Anti-grind rules
These exist because repetitive grinding is the game's failure condition (see [[Game Pillars]]).

1. **Depth pays more than farming.** A Zone 4 run pays about 4x a Zone 1 run. Farming the shallows is never the best plan.
2. **The instructor's Want List.** Each hub visit, the instructor names 3 items or species he pays +50% for. It rotates every run, so the best money is always somewhere new.
3. **Market fatigue.** Selling more than 5 of the same item in one sale drops that item's price by 10% for each extra one (resets next run). Stops shell-fragment farming without punishing normal play.
4. **No empty runs.** Even a wipe in Zone 1 pays the $50 depth bonus, 25% of the haul, and all log entries. Every run moves you forward.
5. **Small stat caps.** The biggest upgrade is +50%. Upgrades help; they never replace skill.
6. **Catch-up rate for friends.** If your total upgrade levels are below the lobby average, you earn +25% Cash ("Rookie Rate") until you catch up. New friends can join a veteran group without feeling useless.
7. **No timers, no energy, no daily login chores.** Weekly seeds and contracts are bonuses, never required.
8. **After maxing.** Once all tracks are maxed, Cash goes into hub upgrades and the "Keep going" endless depth bonus, and Fin Tokens keep flowing. There is always something to spend on.

## 7. First 10 runs player journey
Assumes a group of 2 to 4 friends on Standard difficulty. Solo is similar with the 1.5x payout.

| Run | Where they get to | Cash earned (per player) | What they buy | New thing they see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (tutorial) | Practice sinkhole | $150 | Nothing yet | Every core mechanic, the instructor, Kevin the dummy (see [[Tutorial Design]]) |
| 1 | Zone 1, maybe a peek into Zone 2 | about $250 | Repair tape | First real cave, first rope tangle, first 10 log entries |
| 2 | Zone 2 | about $350 | Flashlight L2 ($200) | First dry chamber with glowworms, first silt room |
| 3 | Zone 2 | about $400 | Lantern L1 ($400) | First run modifier (likely Warm Spring or Low Visibility) |
| 4 | Zone 2, edge of Zone 3 | about $500 | Stamina L2 ($250) | First contract from the instructor; first lantern catch |
| 5 | Zone 3 | about $650 | Tank L2 ($300), Rope Quick Clip ($300) | First Volt Eel, the whole team gets shocked through the rope |
| 6 | Zone 3 | about $800 | Mask Tight Seal ($400) | First fossil worth $$$; species board in camp fills up |
| 7 | Zone 3 (often a wipe) | about $500 | Swim speed L2 ($300) | First earthquake; first total wipe and the "25% kept" lesson |
| 8 | Zone 3, edge of Zone 4 | about $1,000 | Lantern L2 ($800) | First rare biome or rare creature event (guaranteed by run 8, see [[Run Variety System]]) |
| 9 | Zone 4 | about $1,300 | Flashlight L3 ($700) | Halocline Veil, the first real horror moment |
| 10 | Zone 4 | about $1,400 | Tank L3 ($900) | Camp grows to stage 2; the bottom feels reachable |

Target feeling at run 10: "We almost made it. Next time." The bottom should usually be reached for the first time between runs 12 and 18.
