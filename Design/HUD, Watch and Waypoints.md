# HUD, Watch and Waypoints

## Mask HUD
- The HUD is part of the mask.
- Players can customize where each readout sits: depth, distance traveled, pressure, air in tank, stamina, and more.
- Look: greenish-blue glow (a cousin of the creatures' blue bioluminescence), behind a glass layer so it reads as a real display inside the mask.

## Dive watch
- Raise your wrist to check depth and distance down the cave.
- Hologram map: tap the watch and a holographic 3D map shows where you are in the cave, how far down you've gone, and your waypoints.
- Free for now. Possibly a paid upgrade later (see [[Monetization]]).

## Waypoints
- Beacons like Minecraft waypoints, but styled as a glowing light with text.
- You can only place one exactly where you are standing. No placing by coordinates.
- You can name them anything.
- They show up on the hologram map.
