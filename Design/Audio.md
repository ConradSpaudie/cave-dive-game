# Audio

Audio is a top priority, especially breathing.

## Must sound real
- Breathing through the regulator (the most important sound in the game)
- Bubbles
- Muffled voices underwater, clear voices in air pockets
- Rock creaks, earthquakes, cave-ins
- Water currents and flow
- Movement through water
- Spatial audio: turning to face a teammate changes how they sound

## Sourcing
- Free sound libraries first (CC0 sources like Freesound, plus free packs on Fab).
- Conrad can record sounds if needed.
- Check licenses on anything taken from YouTube; most of it can't legally be used.
