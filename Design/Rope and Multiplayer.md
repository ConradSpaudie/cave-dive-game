# Rope and Multiplayer

## Players
- 1 to 4 players. Solo mode and multiplayer mode both exist.
- Friends-only lobbies at launch. Public matchmaking later (voice chat with strangers is why it waits).

## The rope
- One infinitely long rope runs from a stake outside the cave entrance through every player.
- Everyone carries the rope. Players are tied on in an order the team chooses: leader, second, third, last.
- The rope is how players find each other and the way back.
- Tangles: if players swim around each other, the rope tangles. Untangling is a short mini game, about 5 to 7 seconds.
- "Most explored" leaderboard is measured by how much rope was used.

## Joining mid-dive
- Players can join mid-dive. They spawn outside at the stake and follow the rope down to the group.

## Voice
- Built-in proximity voice chat.
- A toggle to turn it off so players can use Discord or FaceTime instead.
- No hand signals; voice only.

## Sabotage (4-player only)
- In 4-player runs, one player can secretly be the saboteur.
- Their goal is to wreck the mission and possibly get everyone killed.
- Open: how the saboteur is chosen, what they can actually do, whether it's optional per lobby, how the others can catch them.
