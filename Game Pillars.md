# Game Pillars

These are the five rules every feature gets tested against. If a feature doesn't serve at least one, cut it.

1. **Friends first.** 85% funny chaos, 15% tense realism and horror. The best moments are the ones you clip and send to people. Reference: PEAK.
2. **Looks and feels real.** Photorealistic in Unreal Engine 5. Priority visuals: lighting, water, rock detail, the diver moving through water, and the environment reacting to you (silt, bubbles, creatures scattering).
3. **The rope connects everything.** One infinite rope ties every player to each other and to the surface stake. It is the map, the lifeline, the joining mechanic, and a source of chaos (tangles, being dragged by currents).
4. **Never repetitive.** Procedurally generated caves, branching paths, depth that keeps changing the rules, creatures and flora to discover and log.
5. **Going down is a choice with a cost.** Air, stamina, panic, injuries, light, and depth effects all push back. Every meter deeper is a decision.

## Tone guardrails
- Gore and jump scares are allowed (cuts on rocks, injuries, cave-ins), but the default mood is funny with friends.
- Fully first person. No third person mode, ever.
- 90% gameplay, 10% story. A short intro explains how the divers got here, then it's up to the players.
